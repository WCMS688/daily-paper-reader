## 摘要
为满足人工智能不断增长的计算需求，高速GPU集群互连正从200 Gbps向400 Gbps演进。我们研究了面向400 Gbps互连的高阶调制与先进均衡技术。推导了4/8阶脉冲幅度调制（PAM）的理论误码率（BER）表达式，以及由32进制正交幅度调制（32QAM）生成的六种PAM6格式的近似表达式。在加性白高斯噪声下，由framed-cross 32QAM生成的PAM6在PAM6格式中具有最佳BER性能。在KP4前向纠错（KP4-FEC）极限下，相对于PAM4，PAM6和PAM8分别带来约4.2 dB和7.2 dB的峰值信噪比（PSNR）代价。仿真在不同带宽限制下比较了线路速率440 Gbps（净速率400G）的PAM4、PAM6和PAM8。随着带宽变得更加受限，带宽需求对性能的影响日益超过噪声容限。在轻度带宽受限信道中，PAM4达到KP4-FEC极限所需的PSNR最低；而在中度受限条件下，PAM6表现最佳。在严重受限条件下，PAM8因其更低的带宽需求和对高频衰减更低的敏感性而具有显著优势。采用最大似然序列估计时，PAM6和PAM8在所有评估的信道条件下均能达到KP4-FEC极限，而PAM6在大多数信道中所需PSNR更低，凸显了其在400G-SerDes系统中的潜力。

## Abstract
High-speed GPU cluster interconnects are evolving from 200 to 400 Gbps to meet the growing computing demands of artificial intelligence. We investigate high-order modulation and advanced equalization for 400 Gbps interconnects. Theoretical bit error rate (BER) expressions for 4/8-ary pulse amplitude modulation (PAM) and approximate expressions for six PAM6 formats generated from 32-ary quadrature amplitude modulation (32QAM) are derived. In additive white Gaussian noise, PAM6 generated by framed-cross 32QAM achieves the best BER performance among the PAM6 formats. PAM6 and PAM8 incur peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR) penalties of approximately 4.2 and 7.2 dB, respectively, relative to PAM4 at the KP4 forward error correction (KP4-FEC) limit. Simulations compare PAM4, PAM6, and PAM8 at a line rate of 440 Gbps (net rate of 400G) under different bandwidth limitations. As bandwidth becomes more constrained, bandwidth requirements increasingly dominate performance over noise tolerance. PAM4 requires the lowest PSNR to reach the KP4-FEC limit in mildly bandwidth-limited channels, whereas PAM6 performs best under moderate limitations. PAM8 offers a significant advantage under severe limitations because of its lower bandwidth requirement and reduced susceptibility to high-frequency attenuation. With maximum likelihood sequence estimation, PAM6 and PAM8 reach the KP4-FEC limit under all evaluated channel conditions, while PAM6 requires a lower PSNR in most channels, highlighting its potential for 400G-SerDes systems.
