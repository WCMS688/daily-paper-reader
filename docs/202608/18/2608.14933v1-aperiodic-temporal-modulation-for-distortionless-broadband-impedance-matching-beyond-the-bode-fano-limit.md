## 摘要
Bode-Fano界限设定了无源、线性、时不变匹配网络中带宽与反射之间的基本权衡。我们证明，非周期时间调制的电抗元件能够模拟匹配给定脉冲所需的非Foster响应，从而实现超越Bode-Fano极限的无反射、近乎无失真的能量传输。该方法引入了一个新的约束：一个随脉冲带宽缩放的最小直流偏置，该约束源于调制电容在所有时刻保持为正的要求。这一调制-偏置界限用带宽-能量权衡取代了经典的带宽-反射权衡。一个实际的电路仿真证实了宽带匹配与波形保真度的保持，表明该方案在物理上是可实现的，而不仅仅是数学上的规避。

## Abstract
The Bode-Fano bound sets a fundamental trade-off between bandwidth and reflection in passive, linear, time-invariant matching networks. We show that an aperiodically time-modulated reactive element can emulate the non-Foster response required to match a prescribed pulse, achieving reflectionless, nearly distortionless energy transfer beyond the Bode-Fano limit. The approach introduces a new constraint: a minimum dc bias that scales with pulse bandwidth, derived from the requirement that the modulated capacitance remain positive at all times. This modulation-bias bound replaces the classical bandwidth-reflection trade-off with a bandwidth-energy trade-off. A realistic circuit simulation confirms broadband matching with preserved waveform fidelity, demonstrating that the scheme is physically realizable and not merely a mathematical circumvention.
