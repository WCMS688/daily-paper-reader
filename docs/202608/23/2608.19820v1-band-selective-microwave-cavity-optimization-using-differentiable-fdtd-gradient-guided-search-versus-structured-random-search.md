## 摘要
我们使用内部开发的基于JAX的可微分FDTD求解器，比较了基于梯度的逆向设计与结构化随机搜索在介电负载微波腔设计中的表现。基准测试固定了材料占比、滤波密度表示、初始化、频带能量目标函数以及实测选择耗时。每个选定设计均在独立的8000步FDTD模拟中进行评估。在四个目标频带、三个指定材料占比和六个随机种子的条件下，梯度优化在所有72组成对比较中均获得了更高的带内频谱能量占比。平均成对改进为0.356，95%成对自助置信区间为0.339-0.374，精确双侧符号翻转p值为0.03125。梯度、腔模、CFL、材料占比和原始输出完整性检查均在推断前通过。在匹配的材料和计算预算下，结果支持梯度优化在该腔体设计基准中的优势。

## Abstract
We compare gradient-based inverse design with structured random search for dielectric-loaded microwave cavities using an in-house JAX-based differentiable FDTD solver. The benchmark fixes material fraction, filtered density representation, initialization, band-energy objective, and measured selection wall time. Each selected design is evaluated in a separate 8000-step FDTD simulation. Across four target bands, three prescribed material fractions, and six seeds, gradient optimization achieves a higher in-band spectral-energy fraction in all 72 paired comparisons. The mean paired improvement is 0.356, with a 95 percent paired bootstrap interval of 0.339-0.374 and an exact two-sided sign-flip p value of 0.03125. Gradient, cavity-mode, CFL, material-fraction, and raw-output integrity checks pass before inference. Under matched material and computational budgets, the results support an advantage for gradient-based optimization in this cavity-design benchmark.
